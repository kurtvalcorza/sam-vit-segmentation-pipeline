## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) segmented a synthetic 320×240 scene from one click and reported `sample-sanity` `mask_iou` against its self-drawn rectangle. This notebook keeps that scene and its inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real labelled dataset slice fetched with per-row-group pins and turned into prompt-to-mask targets under a stated rule, a corpus-level protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (IoU, hit rate, per class), two prompt-only baselines, a bounded fine-tuning of the mask decoder, a per-class breakdown on both prompts, the scene and four held-out targets re-segmented after adaptation, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 4-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the processor or the model is constructed; `SAMViTSegmentationPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `urllib`, `pyarrow`, `scipy`, `torch.optim` or the model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is the first three row groups of `scene_parsing/validation/0000.parquet` from `zhoubolei/scene_parse_150` at the immutable parquet-conversion revision `e660d866a1351c70bcf07925d2600b60bd6e3bc3` (ADE20K scene parsing, BSD-3-Clause), read over HTTPS range requests (about 14 MB); the shard's declared size and every row group's decoded SHA-256 and byte total are pinned in the carried `samples.py` (`ROW_GROUP_PINS`), and a cached or fetched row group is refused on any mismatch. `read_corpus` makes each image at most one record: `select_target` keeps the largest 4-connected component of one class covering `TARGET_MIN_FRACTION`..`TARGET_MAX_FRACTION` (3..35 %) of the image, `point` = the mask's distance-transform maximum, `box` = its tight bounds, `category` = the ADE20K class name; `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws 180 / 45 / 70 by image and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no image (by decoded-pixel digest) is shared. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the dataset, the revision, the byte count and the licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` + `segment` with sanity checks + `evaluation_report` on the synthetic scene (inference contract) → `box_fill_baseline` + `centre_disk_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with both prompts and the per-class breakdown (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → `segment` + `evaluation_report` on the scene again and the example panels (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `SAMViTSegmentationPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** `iou_scores` are the model's own uncalibrated predictions from an unclipped regression head (values above 1.0 occur) and are used only to rank the three candidates; the corpus IoU and hit rate are measured overlaps against the dataset's masks; no acceptance threshold ships. The scene re-segmented after adaptation carries a `sample-sanity` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the box-fill and centre-disk baselines and the frozen model (point and box prompts) are scored on the same 70 targets as the adapted model, on both measures and per class; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample under one dataset's labelling convention with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly that the frozen point prompt scores below box-fill on these targets and that the gain is measured under ADE20K's whole-region convention only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and image-disjoint; validation (45 targets) selects the epoch on the mean of the point- and box-prompt IoU and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that images from few scenes or sessions must be split by source.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (16), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (4096), `MAX_PROMPTS` (16), `NUM_MULTIMASK_OUTPUTS` (3), `MASK_THRESHOLD` (0.0), the record bounds (8..5,000) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one input refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each image paired with its `<stem>_mask.*`, the click and the box derived from the mask, and the records flow through the same validation, image-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected shape and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/sam_vit_segmentation_train.csv`.
- Licence: the weights are Apache-2.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; ADE20K scene parsing is BSD-3-Clause and is not redistributed — the export records the dataset name, repository, revision, file, licence, shard size and row-group pins under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

## Outputs

The default notebook path writes (under `outputs/`):

- `sam_vit_segmentation_train.csv` — the training split's summary table (id, category, size, mask fraction, prompts, provenance)
- `sam_vit_segmentation_input_manifest.json` — the inference-contract manifest (schema, per-input observations, one recorded rejection finding)
- `sam_vit_segmentation_mask_frozen.png`, `sam_vit_segmentation_mask_adapted.png` — the synthetic scene's best candidate mask before and after adaptation
- `sam_vit_segmentation_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted metrics (point and box) on validation and test, the per-class comparison, the adaptation history
- `sam_vit_segmentation_examples/` — four held-out targets as side-by-side panels (image with click and box, frozen point mask, adapted point mask, target)
- `sam_vit_segmentation_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `sam_vit_segmentation_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, model identity, snapshot, corpus and target rule, inference-contract reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime

The earlier record for the superseded `TASK-INFERENCE` carrier is kept in `../docs/release-verification.md`.
